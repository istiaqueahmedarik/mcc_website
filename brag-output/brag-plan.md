# Brag Plan: MCC Trainer

## What is this app?
MCC Trainer is the teaching operations surface for managing classrooms, live sessions, students, topics, and multi-provider contest reports in one role-aware workspace.

## Planning rubric
1. **App:** A trainer dashboard and classroom workspace for day-to-day competitive-programming instruction.
2. **Strongest claim:** One place to see classrooms and the work needing attention, then move directly into a live teaching or contest-report workflow.
3. **Visual hook:** A dark SF Pro interface whose classroom card becomes the classroom header, followed by a floating liquid-glass dock with a moving optical lens.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The trainer dashboard, a real classroom card, the classroom header and bottom dock, and the contest report's first-positive 24h/48h/72h performance value.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 seconds; enough time to show one complete dashboard-to-insight flow without turning the interface into a feature list.
6. **Tone:** Polished; a quiet premium product film with precise, purposeful motion.
7. **Audio:** A steady low-volume business beat with three restrained UI/impact accents and subtle audio-reactive depth.
8. **Share caption:** Teaching has enough moving parts. MCC Trainer puts classrooms, live sessions, and contest insight in one calm control room.
9. **User flow:** Scan the trainer dashboard → open a classroom → select Contests from the dock → read the freshest meaningful performance signal.

## The angle
Teaching competitive programming has many moving parts; the trainer should not feel them. The video treats MCC Trainer as a calm control room: real operational detail enters in layers, but the trainer's next action stays obvious. Product-specific motion—the classroom card becoming the classroom header and the dock lens following the active destination—carries the story.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On near-black, the line “Teaching has enough moving parts.” resolves beside a restrained field of classroom-status tokens. The word “enough” lands with a quiet blue pulse, not a loud launch cliché.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Recreate the real trainer dashboard with summary chips, “Your classrooms,” a searchable card for Algorithms — Batch 07, and a bounded “Needs attention” rail.

- Reveal the real labeled liquid-glass dock—Updates, Live, Topics, People, Contests, More—and move its lens to Contests.
- Resolve on a compact contest report row where a single “+3 · 24h” value appears under Performance, with the explanatory 48h/72h logic kept visually secondary.

## Outro / punchline
“One calm place to teach.” settles above MCC Trainer and the MCC mark.

## User flow worth showing
Trainer dashboard → open Algorithms — Batch 07 → choose Contests from the classroom dock → see the first positive recent-performance window.
